## 8) FAQ Candidates (for schema)
- Q1: How do I know if my company needs a custom CRM?
- A1: If your sales process requires unique stages, complex approvals, cross-team workflows, and integrations that standard CRMs cannot handle without heavy workarounds, a custom CRM may be the right option.
- Q2: Can we start with a standard CRM and migrate later?
- A2: Yes. Many companies begin with standard tools and then move to hybrid or custom systems as process complexity and reporting demands increase.
- Q3: How long does a custom CRM project take?
- A3: A phased rollout usually takes 3 to 6 months depending on scope, integrations, and process maturity.
- Q4: What is the biggest mistake in CRM projects?
- A4: Implementing technology before clarifying process ownership, data standards, and success metrics.
